Frequently Asked

Everything You Need To Know

How do I verify the authenticity of my bottle?

Every bottle in our vault includes factory sealed cellophane packaging with laser-engraved batch codes on the base of the glass bottle. You can cross-reference your batch code on global cosmetic checkers or request a certificate of origin from our concierge.

What are the delivery times for Cash on Delivery?

We dispatch all orders within 12 hours via express courier. Delivery takes 24–48 hours for major hubs (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi) and 48–72 hours for all other cities across Pakistan.

What is your 200% authenticity refund guarantee?

If any bottle purchased from Perfume Daddy is proven to be unauthentic by an authorized brand distributor, we will issue a full 100% money-back refund plus an additional 100% store credit store guarantee.

Do you offer custom corporate gift packages?

Yes, we curate custom luxury gift boxes with bespoke ribbon wraps and personalized handwritten calligraphy cards for weddings, corporate galas, and festive holidays.
